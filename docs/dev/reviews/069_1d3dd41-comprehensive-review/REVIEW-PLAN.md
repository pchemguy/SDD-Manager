Record narrower depth or an unassessed concern explicitly rather than treating structural validation as content/behavior coverage.

Closed campaign contents, retained AI logs, generated artifacts, unrelated checkouts and external linked projects are excluded. Preserve them through Git path/object checks. Source-only files are assessed for their own audience, not copied into runtime guidance.
